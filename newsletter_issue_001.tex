\section{01. Hilton’s Agentic Shift: "Conrad" Moves Beyond Chat}
\textbf{Quick Take:} Hilton has deployed an agentic workflow layer across its luxury portfolio, giving AI "negotiation authority" for B2B contract terms based on live occupancy data. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} We are moving from "Search and Book" to "Delegate and Confirm."

\section{02. Vellum Secures \$150M for B2B Reconciliation AI}
\textbf{Quick Take:} Series C funding closed to scale the "Invisible Back-Office" suite, automating complex multi-currency reconciliations for DMCs. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} Profitability in 2026 is found in the elimination of administrative friction.

\section{03. India’s "Heritage Digital Twin" Mandate}
\textbf{Quick Take:} The Ministry of Tourism announced a mandate for 3D digital twins of all Grade-A heritage sites to enable B2B "Virtual Recce" and AR tours. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} Heritage is now a data asset. DMCs must curate digital layers as well as physical ones.

\section{04. Google Releases A2A (Agent-to-Agent) Protocol}
\textbf{Quick Take:} Gemini 3 Flash now supports a native protocol allowing a travel agent's AI to speak directly to a hotel's management AI. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} The winning platforms will be "AI-readable," not just "human-readable."

\section{05. Amadeus Acquires Flux-Predict for B2B Logistics}
\textbf{Quick Take:} Amadeus integrated "Predictive Delay Mitigation," using frontier models to reroute ground transport hours before a delay occurs. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} Reliability is the new luxury. Solve the problem before the guest feels it.

\section{06. NVIDIA NIM Integration in Marriott Global Centers}
\textbf{Quick Take:} Marriott is using NVIDIA’s Inference Microservices for real-time translation and sentiment analysis in 45 languages for B2B partners. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} Compute infrastructure has replaced the traditional multilingual workforce requirement.

\section{07. The Emergence of "Travel Graph" Portability}
\textbf{Quick Take:} A new decentralized memory layer allows traveler preferences to follow the user across different B2B platforms securely. \\
\textbf{\color{accentred}The Insight:} Personalization is moving from "What did they buy?" to "Who are they?"

The overarching theme today is the \textbf{Death of the Interface}. We are witnessing a transition where the primary "user" of travel-tech is no longer a human clicking buttons, but an agent managing a workflow. For B2B leaders, the strategy must shift from building "user-friendly apps" to building \textbf{"agent-ready ecosystems."} Making the complex infrastructure of travel invisible is the only way to make the human experience feel like magic.
